Demo 2: USS Styles

This demo demonstrates how to use Unity Style Sheets (USS) to customize the appearance of VirtuaStick.
A sample button lets you switch between predefined USS themes in real-time, showing how easily VirtuaStick adapts to different visual styles.

You can use this demo to verify how USS affects:
- Stick background and handle visuals
- Borders and outlines
- Overall look and feel across various game themes

This is ideal for UI designers who want to integrate VirtuaStick seamlessly into their project’s aesthetic.
